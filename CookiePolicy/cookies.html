Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how TheUrbanNet LTD (“TheUrbanNet”, “the Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on the TheUrbanNet waitlist website at waitlist.theurbannet.com.

In short: this website does not set any cookies. It stores one small item in your browser to remember your answer to our cookie banner, and it only uses analytics if you choose “Accept”.

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we use personal data more broadly. We aim to comply with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and guidance from the Information Commissioner's Office.

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device, such as a computer, smartphone, or tablet. Websites can also use similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, to remember information between visits.

The law treats these technologies in the same way as cookies, so this policy covers both.

What We Use

Your Cookie Choice (Strictly Necessary)

When you press “Accept” or “Decline” on our banner, we save your answer in your browser's local storage so the banner does not appear on every visit.

  • Name: theurbannet-cookie-consent
  • Value: “accepted” or “declined”
  • Kept until: you clear your browser data

This item exists only to remember your choice. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it does not require consent under PECR.

Analytics (Only If You Accept)

If you press “Accept”, we load Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and understand how people find and use the waitlist. It does not set cookies, does not use local storage, and does not track you across other websites.

It tells us, in aggregate:

  • Which pages are visited, and how often
  • Which website or link brought visitors here
  • The country, browser, and type of device used
  • How quickly pages load

We cannot use this information to identify you. If you press “Decline”, or do not answer the banner, analytics is never loaded.

What We Do Not Use

This website does not use:

  • Advertising or targeting cookies
  • Social media cookies, pixels, or embedded social content
  • Login or account cookies

The social media icons in our footer are plain links. Those platforms may set their own cookies once you visit them, under their own policies.

Other Services Your Browser Contacts

These services do not set cookies on this website, but your browser connects to them, so they receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type:

  • Cloudflare: hosts and delivers this website, and protects it from attacks.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts used on our pages.
  • Google Apps Script: receives your answers when you submit a waitlist form. Our Privacy Policy explains how we use them.

Your Choices

When you first visit our home page, a banner asks whether we may use analytics. You can:

  • Press “Accept” to allow analytics
  • Press “Decline” to refuse it

You can change your mind at any time by selecting “Cookie Settings” at the bottom of our home page. If you decline after previously accepting, analytics stops from the next page you open.

You can also clear this website's data in your browser settings. This removes your saved choice, and the banner will appear again.

Data Protection

Where any of these technologies involve personal data, that data will be processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy .

Changes to This Policy

We will update this Cookie Policy if we change the technologies we use, for example when the full TheUrbanNet platform launches. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
